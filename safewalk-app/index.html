    Welcome to SafeWalk

    Rate streets and areas as safe or unsafe, find the safest route, and alert someone fast if you need to. Ratings you add are shared with everyone using SafeWalk — but never your name.

    📍

    Tap to rate a spot

    Tap anywhere on the map to mark it safe or unsafe. Press and hold instead to grow a circle and mark a whole area.

    🛣️

    Mark a whole street

    Choose "Whole street" when rating, then tap along the road — the start, then each turn — to trace the stretch you mean. It can run through several streets in a row, and Undo takes back the last tap.

    🧭

    Find the safest route

    Get walking or biking directions that favor streets rated safer by the community — then tell us how it actually felt afterward.

    🆘

    SOS & My Page

    The SOS button calls your emergency contact directly, after you confirm. Your own reports, that contact, and your settings all live under My Page, at the bottom right.
