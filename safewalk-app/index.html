      What SafeWalk knows about you

      Shown to everyone

      Your street and area ratings, and any note you attach to them. Your incident reports: what happened, where, and roughly when. Never your name, email or account — there is no way for anyone to see who made a mark, or to group marks by who made them.

      Only you can see it

      Your email address. Which places you have voted on. Your reporter standing and the record of who agreed with your marks. Nobody else can read any of it.

      Kept only on this phone

      Your emergency contacts and their numbers — these are never uploaded anywhere. Your theme and settings, the last map you loaded, and any marks waiting to upload.

      While someone is watching your walk

      Only your latest position is stored, replaced each time it updates — there is no record of the path you took. Anyone holding the link can see it until the walk ends, and the whole thing is deleted after twelve hours.

      How old things look to other people

      Other people see your ratings dated by the day, and incident reports by the hour — never the minute. That is deliberate: exact times let anyone reading the public map reconstruct a person's walk home from a line of marks.

      Other companies involved

      The map, routing, address search and street data come from OpenStreetMap, Valhalla, Nominatim and Statens vegvesen; your account and marks are stored by Supabase; the app itself is served by GitHub. Those services necessarily see the area you are looking at. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind — the app does not measure what you do.

      Getting rid of it

      Delete any mark from “My reports & marks”. Delete your whole account from Profile: that removes your account, your votes, your incident reports and your walks. Your street ratings stay on the map, permanently unlinked from you, so the warnings other people rely on do not disappear.

      Welcome to SafeWalk

      See what other people have found about the streets around you, plan a route that avoids the worst of it, and let someone follow you home. What you add is shared with everyone using SafeWalk — your name never is.

      📍

      Tap to rate

      Tap the map to mark a spot safe or unsafe. Press and hold to mark a whole area — or choose “Whole street” and tap along the road, turn by turn, to trace the stretch you mean.

      🧭

      Plan it, then walk it

      Get walking or biking directions that favour streets other people rated safer. Then tap “Start walking this route” — two big buttons let you mark how each stretch felt, one press, without looking at the screen.

      👁

      Let someone follow you home

      Send a link to whoever you want. They see where you are and when you arrive — no account, nothing to install. If you stop moving for a long time you get a countdown to cancel before they’re told.

      ⚠️

      If something happens

      Report what happened and where. It stays on the map for seven days, and people who were there can confirm or dispute it. “Near me” also lists the closest places that are open right now — somewhere you can walk into.

      🆘

      SOS & My Page

      SOS calls your emergency contact directly, after you confirm — so set one before you need it. Your reports, that contact, your theme and your account all live under My Page.
